# Steering: Frankenstein Category Context

CryptoScroll targets Frankenstein at Kiroween 2026: a dead drop assembled from spy tradecraft, browser WebCrypto, Cloudflare edge storage, human-readable Markdown and physical QR sharing.

Connect each feature to a concrete benefit: cross-device delivery, deliberate one-time opening, or a separately shared passphrase. A category narrative does not justify weakening encryption or making unsupported claims. AES-GCM is not a one-time pad, and Cloudflare KV does not offer atomic delete-and-return.

Show the contrast in a demo: create on one browser, transfer the QR/link, enter a wrong passphrase without burning, open correctly, then show the second attempt rejected. Explain the metadata/read split and the Durable Object's role.

Use the product spec to agree on behavior before editing, security hooks to catch recurring mistakes, and property tests to explore inputs. Record what Kiro actually implemented, reviewed or tested, with session evidence. Do not attribute Lovable or Codex work to Kiro, invent Kiro Web usage or backdate these artifacts.

Submission also needs a public licensed repository, a working app, a public demonstration video under three minutes and an accurate Kiro write-up. See [the submission guide](../../docs/ref/submission.md) for verified requirements and outstanding evidence.
